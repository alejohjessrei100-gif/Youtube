What this is: the full animation workflow behind a Roblox story-Shorts channel (blocky characters, emotion faces
with lip sync, detailed colourful sets, continuous voice-over, punchy camera, meme SFX). Your Claude does the
animating; your PC (Blender) does the rendering. You record the voice and approve the stills.

No character from the original channel is included — you get your OWN hero (your Roblox avatar, or one Claude
builds from your description).

SETUP (once, ~15 minutes)
1. Install Blender 5.0 or newer from blender.org.
2. Install ffmpeg: open PowerShell and run   winget install Gyan.FFmpeg     (or skip it if you have CapCut installed).
3. Extract this zip, rename the extracted RobloxShortsKit folder to  RobloxAnim  and move it into Documents
   (so you get Documents\RobloxAnim\base_rig.blend, faces2\, queue\ ...).
4. Make a folder Documents\RobloxAnim\sfx and put some short meme sound effects in it (.mp3) — whoosh, pop,
   vine boom, ding, crickets, bonk, bruh, gulp, dun-dun-dun, yippee, sad trombone, scream, gasp ...
5. Double-click  Documents\RobloxAnim\queue\START_RENDER_QUEUE.bat  and leave that window open while you want renders.
6. Best experience: use the Claude desktop app and link the chat to your computer, so Claude can drop render jobs
   and pick up the results by itself. Without the link it still works — Claude sends you files and tells you where
   to put them.

FIRST CHAT
Open a new Claude chat, upload this same zip (RobloxShortsKit.zip), and send:

   Set up my Roblox Shorts studio from this kit. Read SKILL.md and follow it.

Claude will ask whether you already have a character model, build/import your hero, and show you test pictures.

EVERY VIDEO AFTER THAT
Easiest: keep the zip in a Claude Project (Project files) and start each video chat inside that project.
If your chat is linked to your computer you can also just say:
   Read Documents\RobloxAnim\SKILL.md — new video.
(your approved hero is saved on your PC as hero.json, so it stays the same in every chat).
Then send your voice recording (video or audio) + the script text and say "new video". Claude cuts the pauses out of
your voice, writes the animation, sends stills for approval, then renders the final 1080p + 4K into
Documents\RobloxAnim\Final Videos.
