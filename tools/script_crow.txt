A chess game in a city park has been going for a year, and nobody's ever seen the other player. Every morning, Mr. Okafor makes one move and walks home. The next morning, a black piece has moved. One piece. Always a good move. He asks the park staff. Not them. He asks the kids. Not them. And he's losing. So one night, he hides behind a tree with a flashlight. At sunrise, a crow lands on the board. It stares for a full minute. Then it pushes a knight with its beak. Checkmate. Mr. Okafor sits down, resets the board, and says, best of fifty. The crow is up eleven to four.
