A museum got broken into eleven times, and nothing was ever stolen. Every time, guards found one painting hanging slightly crooked. A girl in a yellow dress. The cameras showed a man in a hoodie, just standing in front of it, for an hour. Then he'd leave. The twelfth time, they caught him. He was eighty-one. He didn't run. He just pointed at the signature in the corner. It was his. He painted his wife at twenty and sold it for rent money. She passed last spring, and he couldn't afford a ticket to see her. The museum didn't press charges. They gave him a key.
