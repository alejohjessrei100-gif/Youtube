A woman is making soap in her yard in Kentucky. The sky is clear. Then it starts to rain... meat. Chunks of red flesh, some as big as your palm, fall all around her for several minutes. It's March 1876. Neighbors come running. Two of them do the obvious thing: they taste it. Mutton, says one. Venison, says the other. Scientists get samples. One calls it lung tissue. Another finds muscle and cartilage. The best guess today? A flock of vultures flew over, and threw up. Vultures vomit to get lighter and fly off fast, and when one does it, the rest often follow. One chunk is still on display in Bath County.
