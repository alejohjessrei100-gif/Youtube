He had a rival in parliament who hated him, so he did something strange. He asked the man for a favor. Specifically, he wrote him a polite letter asking to borrow a rare book. The rival lent it. He returned it a week later with a thank-you note. And from that day on, the rival was polite to him, then friendly, and finally a friend for life. You see, the man was Benjamin Franklin, and he'd figured something out. When you do someone a favor, your brain has to explain why. And the easiest answer is, "I must like him." Psychologists tested it in 1969, and it held up. So next time someone can't stand you, don't give them a gift. Ask to borrow a pen.
