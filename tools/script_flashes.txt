Every night at nine, a man stood in the hospital parking lot pointing a flashlight at the sixth floor. Three flashes. Then he left. Security thought he was signaling someone. Planning something. So on Friday, a guard walked up behind him and grabbed his arm. The flashlight hit the ground. It was Tom, seventy-six, in his slippers. You see, his wife Ellen was in room 612, and visiting hours ended at eight. Fifty years ago, when they were dating, he flashed his porch light three times to say I love you. The guard looked up. In window 612, a tiny light blinked back three times. The next night, the guard brought his own flashlight. So did the nurses. And the whole sixth floor blinked back.
