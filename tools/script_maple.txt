Every night, a burglar visited this house and stole nothing. He just flipped the doormat upside down. The police shrugged. "Sir, that's not a crime." But Walt, who was eighty, just smiled and said, "Leave it." So the neighbors put a camera on the porch, and it showed Walt himself, every night, flipping his own mat. You see, across the street lived Mrs. Alvarez, and every morning she looked out her window. Upside down meant, I'm fine. A lighthouse for one person. Then one morning, the mat was right side up. She dropped her coffee and ran. Walt had fallen in the kitchen, and had waited all night. Now every house on Maple Street flips its mat.
