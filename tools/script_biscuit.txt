One morning, a whole town woke up to three hundred "Lost Dog" posters for a dog named Biscuit. Nobody in town owned a dog named Biscuit. People called the number. A little girl's voice: "Please just say his name if you see him." Every morning, the posters moved to a new street, always in a long loop. Then a mail carrier saw him: an old golden retriever, walking slowly, bumping into a mailbox. Biscuit wasn't lost. He's fourteen and blind, and he still takes his walk every day. The girl put the posters along his route so strangers would say his name, and he'd follow their voices home. Now the whole town says, "Hi, Biscuit."
