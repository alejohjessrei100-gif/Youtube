A lighthouse turns itself on every night at exactly 9:14. It hasn't had a keeper in a year. The old keeper, Walt, retired to town. The coast guard checks the wiring. Fine. They change the lock. It still turns on. 9:14, every night. There are feathers by the switch. So Walt climbs the stairs one last time to watch. At 9:13, he hears flapping. A gull lands on the railing, waddles inside, and pecks the big brass switch. The light comes on. For twenty years, Walt flipped that switch at 9:14, then fed a gull his sandwich crust. The gull never stopped showing up. It just stopped getting paid. Walt reaches in his pocket. He brought bread.
