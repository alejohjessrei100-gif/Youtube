A fisherman sits in a small boat on a Louisiana lake. The water around him starts to move. The boat tips. He looks toward the oil rig, and watches it lean, and go under. It's 1980. A drill crew has just punched a fourteen-inch hole through the lake bed. Beneath the lake is a salt mine, with miners inside. The lake pours in. The hole becomes a whirlpool. It swallows the rig, then a row of barges, then part of an island. The canal that feeds the lake starts flowing backwards. Every miner gets out. Nobody dies. Then the hole fills, and the lake comes back, deeper than before.
