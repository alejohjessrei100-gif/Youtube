New gravestones keep appearing in a town cemetery. One every night. For people nobody's ever heard of. Thomas Reed. Ellis Mae Carter. Jonah Hale. No bodies under them. No records. The town thinks it's a prank. Then a sheriff checks the names against old newspapers. Every one of them was a crew member of the Margaret Ann, a fishing boat that sank off the coast in 1931. Twenty-two men. Never found. Never buried. That night, the sheriff waits by the gate. A woman pulls up with a stone in her truck. She's a stonemason. Her great-grandfather was the captain. He was the only one who made it home. Twenty-two graves. She's got two left.
