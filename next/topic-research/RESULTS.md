# What people actually watch (research run 2026-11-15)

## Speaking rate (why the Beirut video felt slow)
- Reference channel @ricksclips3000, four narrated Shorts: 3.30, 3.55, 3.67, 3.76 words per second (198 to 226 wpm). Median gap between words 0.24 to 0.30 s.
- Beirut film as delivered: 103 words in 37.2 s = 2.77 words/s (166 wpm). That was your 1.15x voice plus about 2.4 s of pauses I added to line sentences up with the beat grid.
- Fix: `plan/voice_fast.sh` trims silences over 0.18 s to about 0.09 s, then speeds up. At 1.3x the same voice is 28.5 s = 3.61 words/s (217 wpm). At 1.4x it is 26.5 s = 3.89 words/s.

## Broad crawl (79 channels found by search, 59 with 12+ Shorts, 2,251 Shorts)
Weak signal. The search turned up many gaming and comedy channels, so theme medians sit near 1.0x. What held up: "How/Why" mechanism titles (explainers about traffic, crosswalks, intersections: 1 to 7M views each) and short surprising facts do well; survival, disaster and mystery titles were below average (0.74x to 1.09x of each channel's own median).
