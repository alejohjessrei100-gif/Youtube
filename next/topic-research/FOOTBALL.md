# Football demand check (2026-11-15)

Method: 46 football channels found by search (20k to 5M subscribers), 43 with 12+ Shorts (1,675 Shorts), plus a YouTube search check of 30 specific stories. View counts are lifetime. Nothing here shows impressions or retention. Stories are not fact-checked yet.

## What football Shorts the data says win
- Rule and mechanic explainers: "Keeping In Play Explained" 32M, "Knee Slide Celly Explained" 7.1M (@ZTHShorts, 1.15M subscribers).
- Single human moments: Bert Trautmann playing on with a broken neck, best Short 17.9M. Maradona's Hand of God, best Short 22.1M.
- "Why" origin stories of football things: why Brazil wears yellow, best Short 4.6M.
- Rejected-then-famous player journeys: 5 to 6M ("Mocked for His Looks, Rejected by Madrid..." 5.1M).
- Skill tutorials and AI hype edits also get tens of millions, but those are other lanes.
- Theme medians against each channel's own median stay near 1.0x. Player names, World Cup and goalkeeper titles are slightly above. Penalty and transfer titles are slightly below. Weak signal.
- Spanish-titled channels: only 2 in the sample, too few to compare with English (41).
