With fifteen minutes left in the FA Cup final, the goalkeeper is knocked out cold. There are no substitutes. So Bert Trautmann gets up and keeps playing. He makes save after save, and Manchester City win 3–1. Years earlier, twenty thousand people had protested when City signed a German who'd been a paratrooper in the war. Now he collects his medal with his neck at a strange angle. A hospital says it's just a crick. Three days later, an X-ray shows five dislocated vertebrae, and one of them cracked in two. Another vertebra had wedged against the crack. It may have saved his life.
