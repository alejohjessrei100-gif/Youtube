Brazil's famous yellow shirt didn't exist until 1954. Before that, they wore white, with blue collars. Then they lost the 1950 World Cup at home, in the Maracanã, and the colors were criticized for not being patriotic enough. So a newspaper held a contest: design a kit with all four colors of the flag. The winner was a nineteen-year-old from Pelotas, Aldyr Schlee. Yellow shirt, green trim, blue shorts. Brazil first wore the new colors against Chile in March 1954, and have worn them ever since. Schlee died on November 15th, 2018, the day before Brazil played Uruguay in London.
