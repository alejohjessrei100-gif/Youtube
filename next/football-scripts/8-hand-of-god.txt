The ball drops in the box. Diego Maradona rises, and punches it into the net. It's the 51st minute of a World Cup quarter-final, Argentina against England, four years after the Falklands War. The referee gives the goal. By Maradona's own account, the linesman never saw the hand. Four minutes later, he picks the ball up in his own half and runs sixty yards in ten seconds through the English team, to score again. The Goal of the Century. Argentina win 2–1. Later, he called the first one a symbolic revenge.
