A World Cup qualifier ends 3–2 in extra time. El Salvador beat Honduras. Seventeen days later, El Salvador's army attacks. The war lasts about a hundred hours, and people call it the Football War. But football didn't cause it. Land did. In El Salvador, five percent of farms held seventy percent of the land, so many Salvadorans crossed into Honduras to work. In the days after the second match, El Salvador said twelve thousand of them had been forced to flee back. The ceasefire came on the night of July 18th. The soldiers left in August.
