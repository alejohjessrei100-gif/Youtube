Italy are one miss from losing the World Cup, and the man walking to the spot is their best player. Roberto Baggio. It's the first World Cup final ever decided on penalties, and the only one that stayed 0–0 after two hours. Brazil lead 3–2. Baggio has hardly touched the ball all day, carrying an injury from earlier in the tournament. His only real chance came in extra time, a shot from twenty-five yards that was pushed over. Now he needs to score to keep Italy alive. He runs up. And the ball goes over the bar.
