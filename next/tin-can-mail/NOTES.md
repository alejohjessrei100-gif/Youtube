## Deliberately left out (conflicting or unverified)
- **When it started:** 1882 (Greene, Savage) against "by 1912" (Wikipedia). The script gives no start date.
- **A ten-kilometre trench drop** (Savage): I doubt it, so I did not use it.
- **The blog's claim** that the 1931 swimmer confessed to tampering with the water supply: uncited legend.
- **Pauline Hoeft** (the only woman to swim the mail; NZ champion swimmer): sources disagree on "world" vs "New Zealand" records. Good for a later beat.
- **Charles Ramsay** (112 swims), the **1946 eruption evacuation** and the **1983 airport** that ended the service.

## Representation
The swimmers were mostly Tongan islanders (Savage found no record of their names; Ramsay is described as the only white
man known to swim). Show Tongan swimmers, not a default outsider protagonist. The unnamed swimmer is a deliberate choice.
