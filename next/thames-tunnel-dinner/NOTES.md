# Short: "The Dinner Party Under the Thames" (Thames Tunnel, 1827–1843). SCRIPT ONLY: nothing is built or rendered.

## Why this story and angle
Documented by a contemporary eyewitness. The shipworm angle is cloned by about five faceless videos (3 to 135 views), so
I avoided it. Nobody I found uses the banquet. Its structure is the one measured on the reference channel: an odd scene
with a puzzle ("why a dinner?"), ridicule ("never hold water"), a hidden reason revealed at about 60% of the words,
a cost (the second flood) and a vindication image (trains still run through it).

## Left out on purpose
The shipworm story (cloned), Duke of Wellington (sources conflict on whether he attended), the locked emergency door and
the contractor Beamish (accounts differ), and any invented dialogue.

## Visual honesty notes (for later)

50 in others): the script gives none.
