# Reverse-engineering @ricksclips3000 (2026-11-15)

Data: the channel's 152 public Shorts (titles and view counts), the 30 recent explainer Shorts in detail, 4 transcripts, and YouTube's public thumbnail frames of the top 6. Downloading the videos themselves is blocked by YouTube's bot check, so the edit pace comes from transcripts and frames, not a frame-by-frame count.

## The numbers
- The explainer era: 30 Shorts, median 8.7M views. The 120 older comedy clips mostly get 50k to 1M. The switch to explainers is what made the channel.
- Topic buckets (median views): Japan 16.5M (2 videos), history (war, ships, medieval, ancient) 9.7M (8), gambling and lottery 8.0M (6), transport and engineering 7.8M (4), psychology and brain 6.7M (7), crime and cons 5.6M (3).
- Title formulas (median views): "Why ..." 9.6M; "X vs Y" or "Levels of X" 9.7M; "The X That/Who ..." 6.8M; "What If / Would You" 5.5M; "How ..." 4.8M. Titles are about 6 words.
- Narration: 3.3 to 3.8 words per second (198 to 226 wpm), median gap between words 0.24 to 0.30 s. Lengths 30 to 66 s.

## The look
- 2D cartoon people (round heads, dot eyes, thin limbs) placed inside rich, lit 3D sets: a castle wall at night, a throne room, a railway, an office, a pirate deck, a Japanese prize counter.
- Moody lighting: spotlights, night blues, warm interiors.
- Captions are one or two small white words, low in the middle of the frame.

## The script
1. Open inside a scene on a person doing something odd ("He..."), with the puzzle in the first sentence.
2. Hold the "why" back until about 55 to 75% of the way through.
3. Doubt or ridicule first, then the clever reason, or the price someone paid.
4. Short sentences (median 9 to 12 words). Ends on a concrete image, not a moral.

## What this means for us
- The topic should be a strange real mechanism, rule, loophole or trick, explained. Not an obscure story.
- Use "Why ..." titles.
- Voice at about 3.6 words per second.
- Our toon 3D look is close to theirs, so the style carries over.
