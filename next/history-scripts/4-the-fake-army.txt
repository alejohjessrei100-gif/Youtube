Spring, 1944. German intelligence is sure the Allied invasion will hit Calais. They've found a whole American army group in England, aimed right at it, led by George Patton, the general they rate highest. There's one problem. That army group barely exists. Its radio traffic is fake. Its landing craft are dummies. And the Germans' best source on it is their own spy, Garbo, who is secretly working for the British. So why build an army that can't fight? For the day after. When the real landings hit Normandy, the story was already waiting: this is just the distraction, the main attack is still coming at Calais. The plan was to make German reserves wait there, for an army that was never going to land.
