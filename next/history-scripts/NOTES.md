# History-mechanism scripts (open-loop structure), original writing
Each opens inside a scene with a puzzle, states the open question out loud, answers it after the middle, and ends on an image. Single paragraphs for ElevenLabs. About 30 to 35 s at 3.6 words/s.

Sources: Wikipedia "Line infantry" and "Musket" (smoothbore veer, effective range under about 80 m, volleys compensated, rifled muskets reached several hundred yards and officers kept dense lines); "Food taster", "Mithridatism", "Bezoar" (praegustator, Halotus and Claudius "allegedly", Mithridates VI and his father's poisoning, Paré's test on a condemned cook who died seven hours later); "1883 eruption of Krakatoa" (10:02 a.m. explosion, RMS Norham Castle eardrums at about 64 km, Batavia gasworks gauge 160 km off the scale, windows and walls, Perth 3,110 km, Rodrigues 4,800 km, the pressure wave circled the globe more than three times); "Operation Fortitude" (FUSAG under Patton "whom the Germans held in high regard", fake wireless traffic, dummy landing craft, the double agent Garbo, the post-landing story of a diversion before Calais).

Flags: "two hundred years" is rounded (late 1500s to the Napoleonic Wars and after). Claudius's poisoning is "alleged". Paré's being "French royal surgeon" is general knowledge; the page says "the King's court". In the Fortitude script the last line states the aim ("the plan was to make German reserves wait"), not a measured result. FUSAG was not mainly dummy tanks (the article says so), so the script does not claim inflatable tanks; those belong to the separate Ghost Army.
