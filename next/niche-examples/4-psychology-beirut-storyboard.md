# Script 4 storyboard: Beirut camp

55 BPM camera grid, one beat = 1.091 s. 18 shots, 35 beats = 38.2 s (voice ends at 37.2 s, then about one beat of hold). 9:16.
Voice: `4-psychology-beirut-voice_1.15x.mp3`, with the pauses between sentences nudged so each sentence starts on a beat (plan in `4-psychology-beirut-beatplan.json`). A visual version with framing sketches is `4-psychology-beirut-storyboard.html`. Nothing is built or rendered.

## Shots

### Shot 1  ·  beats 0 to 2  ·  0.00 to 2.18 s  ·  Beirut
- **Voice:** "He splits eighteen boys into two teams,"
- **Camera:** Low three-quarter dolly-in along the line, 2 beats
- **Picture:** Camp field. Eighteen boys stand in one long line facing camera. The researcher is in the foreground with a clipboard, pointing along the line. The number 18 is on screen from frame 0.
- **On screen:** 18 (ticks 1 to 18 on the word 'eighteen')
- **Sound:** 55 BPM pulse starts on frame 0. Counter ticks. Camp ambience: far-off kids, cicadas.

### Shot 2  ·  beats 2 to 4  ·  2.18 to 4.36 s  ·  Beirut
- **Voice:** "at a summer camp in Beirut."
- **Camera:** Overhead crane-up with a slight rotate, 2 beats
- **Picture:** Field from above. The researcher drags a chalk line across the middle. The boys drift into two clumps, one each side. Beat 3 to 4 is a held beat of silence on the split.
- **On screen:** BEIRUT (lands on beat 3, the word 'Beirut')
- **Sound:** Chalk-drag swish. Pulse continues. Ambience dips for the held beat.

### Shot 3  ·  beats 4 to 6  ·  4.36 to 6.55 s  ·  Beirut

- **Camera:** Top-down, slow push, 2 beats
- **Picture:** The eighteen boys stand in a tidy grid seen from above, all in plain clothes. On beat 4, ten of them get a small floating cross badge. On beat 5, the other eight get a crescent badge. Badges are flat white discs with an ink outline.
- **On screen:** 10 + cross badge (beat 4); 8 + crescent badge (beat 5)
- **Sound:** One rising pluck per beat. Soft pop for each badge.

## Rules

- 9:16, 1080 by 1920. Chips and captions stay out of the bottom 20% and the right 12%.
- The researcher is one unnamed adult. Boys use the kid style with six variations. Red Genies in red bibs, Blue Ghost in blue.
- Religion shown only by floating cross or crescent badges (flat white discs, ink outline). Badges appear in shots 3, 4, 5, 6, 13, 14 and 17, never in shot 18.
- Cuts and moves start on beats. Moves run 2 to 4 beats, 1-beat shots are hits. No whip pans.
- Flashback grade (shots 8 to 12): desaturated and warm, vignette, no film grain.
- Count checks to automate when built: 18 boys in Beirut, 22 in Oklahoma, 10 crosses and 8 crescents, each team 5 + 4.
