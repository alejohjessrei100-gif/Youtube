He splits eighteen boys into two teams, at a summer camp in Beirut. Ten are Christian. Eight are Muslim. So he mixes them. Five Christians and four Muslims on the Red Genies. Five and four on the Blue Ghost. He's repeating an American experiment. In Oklahoma, researchers posing as camp staff took twenty-two boys who'd never met, split them in two, and made the teams compete. The boys ended up enemies. But these boys arrive with a label the Oklahoma boys never had. So which line will they fight along? The competitions begin. Fighting breaks out. Not Christians against Muslims. Red against Blue.
