Shots

Look and rules
  • Format. 9:16, 1080 by 1920. Chips and captions stay out of the bottom 20% and the right 12%, where the app buttons sit.
  • Characters. The researcher is one unnamed adult in the shipped man style, with a clipboard. The boys use the kid style with six variations of hair, skin and clothes. Red Genies wear red bibs, Blue Ghost wear blue. In the flashback the boys wear plain 1950s clothes and the adults wear matching camp polos.
  • Religion badges. Flat white discs with an ink outline and a cross or crescent. Nobody is told apart by clothing or looks, only by badge. Badges exist in shots 3, 4, 5, 6, 13, 14 and 17, and never in shot 18.
  • Camera. Cuts and moves start on beats. Moves run 2 to 4 beats, and 1-beat shots are hits. No whip pans, and no move longer than 4 beats. The first frame is already the hook: 18 boys, one man, the number 18.
  • Flashback grade. Shots 8 to 12 only: desaturated and warm, a slight vignette, no film grain, so nothing flickers. A hard cut back to full colour at beat 23.
  • Captions. Word-timed, burned in, in the middle of the lower half, one line at a time, with the current word highlighted.
Sound
  • No music. A soft pulse on every beat at 55 BPM, thinner during the flashback.
  • Beds: Beirut is cicadas, distant kids and warm wind. Oklahoma is dry wind and crickets, band-limited.
  • Hits: chalk swish (shots 2, 10), badge pops (3, 13), name chimes (5, 6), whistle (15), thud (16), X stamp (17).
  • Mix chain as in the earlier film: effects duck 3.5 dB under the voice, limiter, master to about -14 LUFS.

Decisions for you
  • 55 BPM or lower. 55 needed the least pause stretching: the voice runs 37.2 s instead of 34.8 s. At 50 BPM it would run about 39.6 s.
  • Badges or words. Cross and crescent discs keep the frame clean. If you would rather use the plain words "Christian" and "Muslim" on small chips, say so.
  • The fight. It is a dust cloud with no faces, to keep children fighting soft. Tell me if you want it even less literal, for example only the rope dropping and a cut to the clean red and blue shot.
  • Source check first. The Beirut result rests on one sentence. I would read Diab's paper before building.
