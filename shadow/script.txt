My shadow just quit. He left a note. It said, I'm tired of copying you. Then he walked out the door. Without me. I chased him down the street. He was faster. Shadows never get tired. He started copying other people. He chased a cat. The cat chased him back. Then he did the worst thing ever. He joined my mom. Now my mom had two shadows. And they were both mad at me. I had to win him back. So I did his job. I copied him. He jumped, I jumped. He spun, I spun. He danced, I danced. In the middle of the street. Everyone was watching. It was so embarrassing. But he started laughing. A shadow laugh. It sounds like a sneeze. He walked back and stuck to my feet. Best friends again. That night I turned off the lights. And he was gone. I turned them back on. He was there. Off. Gone. On. There. Then I turned them off again. And something in the dark whispered, Your turn to copy me.
