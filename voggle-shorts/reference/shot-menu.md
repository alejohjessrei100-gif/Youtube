# Shot rules and shot menu

From analysis/reports/03_shots_camera.md. Pick a template (T1–T19) for every shot in the beat table.

## Reusable rules

1. **Pace:** cut a new set-up every **1.4-2.2 s** (target mean 1.85 s, median 1.7 s). Use 0.25-0.75 s inserts for punch words. The longest set-up should not exceed 4.0 s, and anything over 2.5 s must contain a pose or expression swap or in-image motion.
2. **Hook:** the first 3.5 s holds 4 shots of 0.6-1.0 s each. Shot 1 is a graphic composition (top-down, symmetrical, or a strong action), shot 2 has a fast push or zoom-blur, and shot 4 is an insert or reaction on the hook's key noun.
3. **Visual-event rate:** something must change in the picture (cut, pose swap, 2D face swap, grade flip, object appears) at least every **1.3 s** on average.
4. **Size mix per video (±5 points):** LS 28%, MLS 26%, MS 20%, MCU 10%, insert/ECU 12%, ELS 3%, CU 1%. Spy-type (document- and object-heavy) stories push inserts to about 20%.
5. **Angle mix:** eye-level about 50%, low 20-30%, high 15-20%, top-down 10%. Give low angles to whoever dominates the scene. Use high or top-down for victims, crowds, maps and objects on tables.
6. **Size by role:** explanation goes on LS/MLS, reaction on MS/MCU/CU, a number or object on an insert or ECU (top-down or POV hands), a new place on ELS/LS, a gag on MS.
7. **Default camera:** every still gets a **creep-in of +1.5-3% over the shot (about 1.4%/s)**, centred on the face. Never leave a shot fully locked unless the frozen look is the joke.
8. **Push-in:** about 1 in 3 shots. +10-18% over the shot, at 5-10%/s.
9. **Zoom punch:** on punch words, a +15-25% push in **0.8-1.2 s** (>= 15%/s), or a straight punch-in cut to a 1.3-1.6x tighter framing of the same set-up. Use 1 per 8-10 s.
10. **Pull-outs and pans** total no more than 10% of shots. Pans move 8-13% of the frame width per shot.
11. **Location changes** use a **whip or zoom-blur transition of 4-8 frames** (directional blur, about 0.2-0.3 s) on about 7% of shots, always into an ELS/LS establishing view.
12. **In-image motion:** about 70% stills (with a 2D move and pose swaps), 15-20% short animated clips of 1-3 s for physical verbs (walk, fall, gallop, throw, wave, drink, papers flying, animal flapping), 10-15% light motion.
13. **Pose swaps:** generate 2-3 variants of each character still with the same background and light, and swap them on stressed words every 1-1.5 s.
14. **2D face swaps** (eyes and mouth) change on emotion beats, about every 1-2 s in character shots. They are not lip-synced.
15. **Composition:** single subjects centred (x 45-55%), with eyes at **28-38% of frame height**. Headroom 5-15% of height (3-8% in MCU). Faces never enter the 44-56% caption band. The caption may sit over the torso or an object.
16. **OTS/back views make up 15-20% of shots,** with a dark, soft foreground mass covering 20-35% of the width at one edge. Use them for dialogue, "watching" moments and reveals.
17. **Depth of field:** MS and tighter are always shallow (background at roughly f/1.8-2.8 look). Wides and top-down shots are deep.
18. **Numbers become objects:** every spoken quantity is staged as a countable physical build-up (falling or piling objects, pins or markers multiplying in 3-5 steps about 0.3 s apart), plus one scale shot (a person next to the pile) or an ECU on the number word.
19. **Maps:** 1 map insert per 20-30 s of travel or strategy. Aged parchment, top-down, with a red or dotted line or arrows growing over 1.5-2.5 s, a creep-in of 2-10%, and a POV finger or hand allowed.
20. **Imagined or hypothetical shots** get desaturated, monochrome or cool-blue grading and a literal visualisation. Flashbacks repeat an earlier composition in monochrome.
21. **Grade flip:** about 1 per video at the story turn. The grade changes in one frame inside the shot on the turn word (warm to grey-blue), together with a 2D face change.
22. **Passage of time:** a bored-tableau sequence (2-3 shots of 1.0-1.7 s), a top-down walk over seasonal debris, or an aerial on the duration line.
23. **Ending:** an ironic wide payoff (ELS, 2.5-3 s, near-static), then a final MCU of the protagonist looking toward the lens with a smug face, 1.7-3.2 s, under the last line.
24. **Callbacks:** reuse at least one earlier composition (same angle and framing) for the payoff or flashback, so the viewer recognises it.

## Shot-type menu (for script-to-storyboard)

| # | template | size / angle | duration | camera | use when the line... |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Graphic hook tableau** | LS, top-down or symmetrical | 0.6-1.0 s | creep +1-2% | opens the video, or is a "they surrounded him" style beat |
| T2 | **Establishing place** | ELS/LS, high aerial or wide; hero tiny | 1.5-2.6 s | whip/zoom-blur in, creep +2% or pull -3% | names a new place or a duration ("stuck on a tiny island", "38 days") |
| T3 | **Action full-body** | LS/MLS, eye or low | 1.5-3.5 s | creep or 1-3 s animated clip | describes what the protagonist does (walks in, offers, drags, rides) |
| T4 | **Hero low-angle power shot** | LS/MLS, low or worm's-eye | 1.5-3 s | push +10-25% | the protagonist dominates, threatens or decides |
| T5 | **Reaction MCU** | MCU/MS, eye or slight low; shallow DoF | 0.7-2.0 s | creep, or zoom punch on the beat | someone reacts: shock, smugness, sadness, anger |
| T6 | **Punch-in** | the same set-up cut 1.3-1.6x tighter | 0.4-1.0 s | static after the cut | the key word of a joke or insult lands ("insulted", "furious", "fanatical") |
| T7 | **Group reaction / crowd** | MLS, high angle or OTS from the cause | 1.2-2.3 s | creep; pose swap or animated | a group laughs, panics, cheers or is horrified |
| T8 | **OTS dialogue pair** | MS OTS, then reverse OTS | 1.0-2.3 s each | static or creep | two parties negotiate, offer or refuse |
| T9 | **Object insert (POV hands)** | insert/ECU, top-down or high POV | 0.3-2.5 s | push +10-25% or a reveal (lid, page flip) | a specific object is named (ink, guidebook, briefcase, medal, coin) |
| T10 | **Number build-up** | LS or insert; objects fall, pile up or multiply in steps | 1.5-2.8 s | creep or static | a quantity is spoken (pair with T11 or T9 on the number word) |
| T11 | **Scale shot** | MS/MLS, person dwarfed by or hugging the pile | 0.8-2.0 s | static or creep | "more than half a tonne", "took their fifty talents" |
| T12 | **Map route** | top-down parchment insert | 1.4-2.8 s | creep +2-10%, line or arrow grows | travel, invasion plans, "sent him to X", "went to Y instead" |
| T13 | **Imagined / literal visualisation** | LS/MLS, desaturated or monochrome or cool | 1.2-2.5 s | creep | "instead of begging...", "he saw a prison full of money", a claim that is untrue |
| T14 | **Flashback echo** | repeat of an earlier composition, monochrome or dark | 1.5-2.8 s | creep, whip out | "before, he'd been...", "because he'd promised" |
| T15 | **Time-passing tableau** | MLS/MS bored figures, an animal, or top-down walk with leaves | 1.0-1.7 s each, 2-3 in a row | static or creep | "for two whole months", "a few months later" |
| T16 | **Grade-flip turn** | MS on the protagonist; colour changes mid-shot on the turn word | 1.5-2.0 s | push +10-15%, zoom-blur out | the mood reverses ("but he wasn't laughing anymore") |
| T17 | **Back-view walk-off / foreground watcher** | MLS/LS back view or OTS | 1.5-2.6 s | creep or push +5% | a transition beat ("that was until he made his next move"), or watching others leave |
| T18 | **Ironic wide payoff** | ELS, eye; silhouettes or a tableau | 2.5-3.0 s | near-locked | the dark or ironic consequence lands |
| T19 | **Outro smug MCU** | MCU, eye, looking toward the lens | 1.7-3.2 s | locked or creep | the final line or tag ("and that was X being nice") |
