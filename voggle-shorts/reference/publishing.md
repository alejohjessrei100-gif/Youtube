# Publishing pack (`09_publish/publish.md`)

Write after the final render.

## YouTube title options (5)
- First option = the chosen video title (4–8 words, Title Case, one emoji at the end that names the emotion).
- Then 4 alternatives using the other title formulas (reference/script-short.md → Title rules): a "How…", a "Why…", a "He [did X] For [ironic Y]", and a superlative-contrast title.
- Never name the famous person in the title. No clickbait that the video doesn't pay off. ≤ 60 characters.

## Description (~90–130 words)
1. **Line 1:** "The true story behind [the hook in plain words]." (≤ 100 characters, it shows in search)
2. **Paragraph 1 (2–3 sentences):** the setup and the stakes, present tense, without spoiling the ending.
3. **Paragraph 2 (2–3 sentences):** what the viewer will witness, plus the theme ("…explores the cost of vengeance…").
4. **Hashtags line:** 3–5 tags, lowercase, from: the theme (#revenge #kindness #betrayal), #truestory or #history, the era or place (#wildwest #ww2 #ancientrome), and the format (#shorts).

Note any historical liberties honestly. If the story is a legend, say "legend" rather than "true story".
