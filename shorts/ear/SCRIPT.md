## Facts (with sources)
- 1731: the British brig *Rebecca* was boarded by a Spanish *guarda costa* off Cuba; her master, Robert Jenkins,
  said an officer cut off his ear. (Wikipedia, "Robert Jenkins (master mariner)"; Britannica, "War of Jenkins' Ear")
- March 1738: Jenkins was summoned to appear before the House of Commons; the opposition used his story against
  Prime Minister Robert Walpole, who did not want war. (Wikipedia, "War of Jenkins' Ear")
- The often-told detail that he showed the pickled ear in a jar to Parliament is NOT corroborated, so the video
  shows him pointing to his bandaged head in Parliament, and the jar only as a symbol (hook, sting, museum).
- War declared 23 October 1739 (O.S.); the real causes were trade, the asiento and smuggling into Spanish America.
- The name "War of Jenkins' Ear" was coined by the historian Thomas Carlyle in 1858. (Wikipedia; Britannica)
