In 1961, a volcano woke up right behind the only village on the most remote island on Earth.
Lava buried the island's fish factory.
So all 264 people left everything, and sailed ten thousand kilometres to England.
There they got jobs, buses, television. Their kids went to English schools.
But in a secret ballot, the islanders voted one hundred and forty-eight to five... to go home.
In 1963, after the coldest English winter in two hundred years, they sailed back.
To a buried factory, and the same volcano behind their houses.
They rebuilt anyway. They even named their new harbour after the camp in England: Calshot.
