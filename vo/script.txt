In 1864, two ships wrecked on the same cold, remote island, just months apart. Neither crew ever knew the other was there. On the Grafton, five men built a hut, took turns cooking, and shared every job. Forty kilometres away, nineteen men from the Invercauld crawled ashore... and it was every man for himself. After nineteen months, the Grafton crew built their own boat, and three of them sailed it across the open ocean to bring help. All five lived. From the Invercauld, only three survived. Same island. Same cold. Same storms. The biggest difference? How they treated each other.
