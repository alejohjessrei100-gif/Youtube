1. **Hook (0–3 s):** the island and the glowing vent behind cottage roofs; the volcano must be on screen in the first 2 s. Light: a cool morning that turns orange-red. Tag painted on a cottage sign: **1961**.
2. **The leaving (3–8 s):** families with bundles walk down to boats; a ship on the horizon. Light: smoky dusk.
3. **England (8–15 s):** a red-brick close, a bus, a TV glowing in a window, then snow on everything. Light: cold blue-grey. Tag on a road sign: **10,000 KM**.
4. **The choice (15–19 s):** an islander at a frosty window looking at a map; a hand goes up in a meeting. Light: cold interior, one warm lamp.
5. **Payoff (19–22 s):** the boat lands on the island again, the volcano calm behind the village. Light: golden. Last word "volcano" on a slow pull-out.
