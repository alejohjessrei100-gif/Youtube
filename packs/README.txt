c1 ... c15 (no c8): 14 ready-made Roblox avatars for the side cast. See examples/cast_lineup.jpg for who is who.
Use in a video section:  make_side('kid_b', '6', .9)   -> avatar packs\c6 as a character named kid_b at kid size.

Your OWN avatar (the hero, if you import it): export it from Roblox Studio and put it in  packs\c20\20.obj
together with its .mtl and texture .png files (don't rename the .mtl). Then hero.json: "mode": "import", "import_id": "20".
