Tomo tested GTA VI crash physics, and they are insane.
First, he drove a tiny car into a wall.
The bumper folded like paper.
Then he hit a traffic cone.
The cone survived.
The car did not.
So he tried a bigger test: one truck, one hill, zero brakes.
The truck flew past a billboard, spun twice, and landed on its wheels.
Tomo screamed, "Is that even legal?"
His friend said, "Tomo, you are not allowed to drive."
Nobody argued.
You see, every dent is calculated by hundreds of tiny springs, so no crash ever happens the same way twice.
Even the cone follows the rules.
So Tomo restarted and tried the exact same crash.
This time, the cone moved first.
